EVIDENCE & EXPLANATION
======================

Project:           Glaucoma Second Opinion
Team:              Elixir
Hackathon:         HackNex 26
Problem statement: HNX26PSI05, Multimodal Medical Image Intelligence

NOTE: Research prototype. Not a medical device. All patient notes are synthetic.


1. PURPOSE
----------
This document shows how the system reaches each assessment, so a clinician can
check the result instead of trusting a black box. Every finding is tied to a
source: a region of the fundus image or a line of the patient notes. A finding
with neither source is treated as a hallucination and is flagged or removed.


2. EVIDENCE THE SYSTEM PRODUCES
-------------------------------
- Highlighted region
    What:  Heatmap over the optic nerve head showing where the image finding
           comes from
    Where: UI, image panel

- Cited findings
    What:  Each finding points to an image region [image: ...] or a line of
           the patient notes [notes: ...]
    Where: UI, findings list

- Confidence score
    What:  Probability for the assessment ( 95% , 85% etc. )
    Where: UI, header of the result

- Intermediate outputs
    What:  Fields extracted from the notes, then image findings, then the
           final assessment
    Where: <path to logs or debug view>

- Grounding check
    What:  A finding with no image region and no note citation is flagged as
           unsupported
    Where: <file that implements the check>

- Timestamp
    What:  When the analysis ran, saved with each output
    Where: <log file or output field>

- Model used
    What:  Which Gemini model produced each step
    Where: <log or output field>

4. HOW TO READ THE OUTPUT
-------------------------
- Heatmap: warm areas mark the part of the image that most influenced the
  result. It shows where the model looked, not proof of cause.
- Source tags: the clinician can verify each finding against the image or the
  exact note line.
- Confidence score: a probabilistic score, not a diagnosis. A low score should
  prompt human review.
- Normal vs glaucoma cues: a healthy disc has a small cup and a pink, thick
  rim. In glaucoma the cup is enlarged and the rim is thin and pale.


5. LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE
-------------------------
- The patient notes are synthetic and written to match each image's label, so
  they are not clinical ground truth and may make results look better than
  they would be on real records.
- A fundus photograph alone cannot confirm glaucoma. Diagnosis also needs IOP,
  visual fields, OCT and a clinical examination.
- The heatmap shows model focus and does not prove that the highlighted area
  caused the result.
- The dataset is small (101 images, binary labels).

### What each metric means

**Accuracy**
Measures the overall proportion of correctly classified cases.

**Sensitivity**
Measures how effectively the system identifies glaucoma-labelled cases.

**Specificity**
Measures how effectively the system identifies normal cases.

**Grounded-finding rate**
Measures the proportion of generated findings that can be linked to a valid image region or relevant patient-note evidence.
